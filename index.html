About the Clinic

Where Smiles Are Crafted with Care

Dent O Care – Dental, Aesthetic & Implant Centre is one of North Lakhimpur's most trusted dental practices. What sets us apart isn't just our modern equipment — it's the way we genuinely care about every patient who walks through our doors. From routine cleanings to full-smile transformations, we approach each case with the same dedication and precision.

Located on Girls' School Road, our clinic was built with one goal in mind: making quality dental care accessible and completely stress-free. We use the latest techniques to ensure treatment is as painless as possible, and we take the time to explain every step so patients are always comfortable and informed.

Experienced Dental Surgeon

Led by Dr. Kaushik Borpatra, a skilled and patient-focused dental surgeon with expertise across all major dental treatments.

Modern Technology

Our clinic is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic and treatment tools for precise, efficient, and comfortable care.

Patient-First Approach

We listen before we treat. Every patient receives a personalised care plan designed around their specific needs and comfort.
